How can I prevent back pain in my horse?

🚨

If your horse is suddenly lame, will not bear weight, or has a hot, swollen leg or foot, call your veterinarian right away. This page is educational and cannot assess your horse.

Short answer

Check that equipment fits, ease horses into work, warm up before every session, keep the horse fit and well-muscled, and work on rider posture and balance with a qualified instructor or trainer.

Prevention steps

  • Fit. Make sure equipment fits and does not interfere with movement; pinching or rubbing can cause abnormal movement that leads to back pain. Use a professional saddle fitter if unsure (University of Minnesota).
  • Build up gradually. Ease horses into work, especially those that have been off, are young, old or overweight, asking only for work they are conditioned for (Minnesota).
  • Warm up with stretching, bending and loosening (Minnesota).
  • Keep the horse fit and well-muscled, since weak horses are more prone to soft-tissue injury (UConn).
  • Rider balance. Work with a qualified trainer or instructor on posture and balance (Minnesota; UConn).
