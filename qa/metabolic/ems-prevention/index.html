Metabolic & Endocrine · Q&A

Can EMS be prevented?

🚨

If your horse is suddenly sore on its front feet, reluctant to move, or has stopped eating, call your veterinarian right away. This page is educational and cannot assess your horse.

Short answer

Prevention centers on weight and feeding: keep high-risk breeds at a healthy weight, feed to an ideal body condition score, give regular exercise and manage pasture carefully during high-sugar seasons.

UC Davis prevention guidance

  • Keep high-risk breeds — ponies, donkeys, Arabians, mustangs — at a healthy weight.
  • Feed to an ideal body condition score rather than generic guidelines.
  • Manage pasture carefully during high-sugar seasons.

UC Davis also notes that these breeds store energy efficiently, which becomes a problem with plentiful food and little exercise, so regular exercise is part of prevention. The sources reviewed do not describe a way to prevent PPID. See signs of EMS.

Sources
